# EXP-007 — Fixed 20-Session 10Y Rate Regime

Status: **COMPLETE — REJECT**  
Issue: #48

## Frozen question

Could a simple, point-in-time rate-trend regime explain the severe chronological instability seen in EXP-006 before we resort to regime-specific ML models?

The regime was fixed before results:

- `RATES_RISING` when `treasury_10y_change_20 > 0`
- `RATES_FALLING_OR_FLAT` when `treasury_10y_change_20 <= 0`

The predictor used no ML. Each test date received the legally mature historical favorable-entry prevalence for its current rate regime and was compared with the global training prevalence.

## Result

**EXP-007 fails the pre-registered viability gate.**

- mean relative Brier improvement: **-0.0063**
- positive relative-Brier folds: **1/3**
- mean ROC AUC: **0.4990**
- AUC > 0.50 folds: **1/3**
- minimum fold AUC: **0.4432**
- full mature-test regime coverage: PASS
- per-regime training support: PASS
- exact EXP-006 sample hashes: PASS

### Fold metrics

| Fold | Relative Brier | ROC AUC | Training prevalence ordering |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 2024 | -0.0043 | 0.445 | Falling/flat higher |
| 2025 | -0.0162 | 0.443 | Falling/flat higher |
| 2026 YTD | +0.0016 | 0.608 | Rising higher |

The historical ordering of the two regimes itself flips by the 2026 training fold. That is direct evidence that this single rate-direction split is not a stable explanation for the opportunity relationship.

## Decision

`DO_NOT_ADVANCE_RATE_REGIME_UNDER_EXP_007`

Do not change the 20-session rate horizon, zero threshold, or substitute another Treasury variable under EXP-007 after seeing the result.

The next useful test should address **time nonstationarity directly**, rather than search across more regime definitions. A pre-registered rolling/recent training window is a cleaner next hypothesis because it tests whether stale historical relationships are the main problem while keeping the target, features, and model fixed.

V3-019 remains blocked. No champion is selected. Sizing remains 1.00x.
