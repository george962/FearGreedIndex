# STAB-001 — Past-only relationship stability selection

## Result

**REJECT — `DO_NOT_ADVANCE_STABILITY_SELECTOR_UNDER_STAB_001`**

The past-only selector found a materially better **ranking** signal, but the frozen training-CDF probability mapping failed the probability-quality gate.

- mean ROC AUC: **0.573254**
- minimum fold AUC: **0.536749**
- AUC > 0.50 folds: **3/3**
- feature-support folds: **3/3**
- family-support folds: **3/3**
- mean relative Brier improvement: **-0.245410**
- positive relative-Brier folds: **0/3**
- EXP-006 sample hashes matched: **True**

Fold AUCs are approximately **0.627 / 0.537 / 0.556** for 2024 / 2025 / 2026 YTD. This is the first post-DIAG methodology to produce AUC above 0.50 in all three exposed folds. However, Brier quality is worse than the legal training base-rate predictor in all three folds, so STAB-001 fails exactly as pre-registered.

## Interpretation

Do **not** call STAB-001 a passing model or use it for V3-019/sizing. The useful hypothesis generated by this frozen failure is narrower: **past-only relationship stability may recover ordering information, while the raw CDF-to-probability mapping is miscalibrated.** A causal calibration test requires a new methodology ID; STAB-001 thresholds may not be retuned.

2024–2026 YTD remain development evidence only. EVID-001 outcomes were not opened.
