# EXP-009 Checkpoint — Fixed 504-row recent-window adaptation

## Result

**REJECT — `DO_NOT_ADVANCE_RECENT_WINDOW_UNDER_EXP_009`.**

EXP-009 changed only one thing from the frozen EXP-006 random forest: each fold trained on the most recent **504 legally mature observations** instead of the entire eligible history.

Everything else stayed fixed:

- 53-feature Treasury lane
- EXP-006 `favorable_entry_20d` target
- identical RF parameters
- identical realized test dates
- no calibration, weighting, regimes, threshold changes, or window search

## Frozen evidence

- exactly 504 training rows in every fold: **PASS**
- EXP-006 sample hashes: **MATCH**
- mean relative Brier improvement vs recent-window base rate: **-0.1726**
- positive relative-Brier folds: **0/3**
- mean ROC AUC: **0.4632**
- positive-AUC folds: **1/3**
- minimum fold ROC AUC: **0.3883**
- mean AUC improvement vs full-history EXP-006 RF: **+0.1071**
- AUC better than full-history RF: **2/3 folds**
- mean Brier improvement vs full-history RF: **-0.0200**
- Brier better than full-history RF: **0/3 folds**

Fold AUCs:

- 2024: **0.6036** vs full-history **0.6293**
- 2025: **0.3975** vs full-history **0.2819**
- 2026 YTD: **0.3883** vs full-history **0.1571**

Recency reduces the severity of the later ranking failure, but does not restore a usable signal. The 2025 and 2026 folds remain below chance, and probability quality deteriorates in every fold.

## Decision

Do not change the 504-row window under EXP-009. A different adaptation timescale requires a new experiment ID.

The accumulating evidence now points to a broader **concept-drift / conditional-relationship problem**, not merely an overly long training history. The next research step should diagnose which feature-target relationships change across years before another model is trained.

Production state remains unchanged: no champion, V3-019 blocked, sizing `1.00x`.
