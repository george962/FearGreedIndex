# EXP-006 — Opportunity-State Target Reformulation

Status: **COMPLETE — REJECT**  
Issue: #46

## Frozen question

Could the retained 53-feature Fear/Greed + Treasury dataset predict a directly actionable 20-session favorable-entry target more reliably than the earlier exact-return/drawdown formulation?

The primary target was pre-registered before results:

- favorable only when `forward_return_20d >= +2%`
- and `max_drawdown_20d > -5%`
- same next-session executable-entry convention
- same chronological 2024 / 2025 / 2026 YTD folds
- training allowed only after `_forward_20d_known_date`

## Result

**EXP-006 fails the pre-registered viability gate.**

| Model | Mean relative Brier | Positive Brier folds | Mean ROC AUC | AUC > 0.50 folds |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Logistic L2 | -0.669 | 0/3 | 0.352 | 0/3 |
| Random forest | -0.148 | 0/3 | 0.356 | 1/3 |

Sample hashes match exactly across models, so the failure is not caused by different evaluation dates.

### Random-forest fold behavior

- 2024 ROC AUC: **0.629**
- 2025 ROC AUC: **0.282**
- 2026 YTD ROC AUC: **0.157**

The 2024 relationship is useful-looking, but it reverses dramatically in later chronological folds. That makes a single stationary opportunity classifier inappropriate under this formulation.

## Opportunity-state distribution

The underlying states also shift materially:

- 2024: GOOD + EXCELLENT ≈ 55.2%
- 2025: GOOD + EXCELLENT ≈ 41.6%
- 2026 YTD: GOOD + EXCELLENT ≈ 35.0%

The prevalence shift by itself does not explain the failure because the benchmark probability is derived from training prevalence, but it reinforces that market conditions changed materially across folds.

## Decision

`DO_NOT_ADVANCE_OPPORTUNITY_TARGET_UNDER_EXP_006`

Do not tune the +2% / -5% target thresholds or the registered model parameters under EXP-006 after seeing these results.

The most informative finding is the **cross-year sign/relationship instability**. If research continues, use a new experiment ID to test an explicitly regime-aware formulation rather than another stationary model tweak.

V3-019 remains blocked. No champion is selected. Sizing remains 1.00x.
